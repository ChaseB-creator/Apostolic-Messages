Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Confessions of Thomas

Testimony I • The Bedrock Confession of Divinity John 20:28
And Thomas answered and said unto him, My Lord and my God.

The highest, most explicit confession of the absolute divinity and bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ in the Holy Gospels.

Testimony II • Courageous Devotion Unto Death John 11:16
Then said Thomas, which is called Didymus, unto his fellowdisciples, Let us also go, that we may die with him.

Fearless apostolic fidelity, demonstrating that genuine love for Christ chooses to walk with Him even into the shadow of death.

Testimony III • Inquiring the Celestial Pathway John 14:5
Thomas saith unto him, Lord, we know not whither thou goest; and how can we know the way?

Thomas’s searching inquiry which elicited the eternal truth: 'I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.'

Testimony IV • The Demand for Physical Reality John 20:25
Except I shall see in his hands the print of the nails, and put my finger into the print of the nails, and thrust my hand into his side, I will not believe.

The providential skepticism that provided irrevocable empirical evidence of Christ’s bodily resurrection for all ages.

Testimony V • Adoration of the Living Redeemer Acts of Thomas 10
Thou art the comforter of the weary, the hope of the destitute, the physician of wounded souls, and the fountain of eternal righteousness.

Apostolic praise celebrating Jesus Christ as the universal refuge and restorer of broken humanity.

Testimony VI • Proclamation of the Incarnate Light Acts of Thomas 39
I believe in Thee, O Lord Jesus Christ, foretold by the prophets, revealed in the flesh, and raised in majestic power to shatter the dominion of darkness.

Proclaiming the unity of ancient prophecy, holy incarnation, and resurrection power that dispels demonic darkness.

Testimony VII • The Victorious Martyrdom Acts of Thomas 142
Into Thy hands, O Lord Christ, I commit my spirit. Keep my soul blameless, and grant me entrance into Thy eternal Kingdom.

Thomas’s final words on St. Thomas Mount in Mylapore, sealing his witness with a spear and passing into eternal glory.
