Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Invocations of Philip

Testimony I • The Prophetic Fulfillment John 1:45
We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.

Philip’s joyful testimony that all covenant scriptures and ancient prophecies find their living culmination in Jesus Christ.

Testimony II • The Evangelical Summons John 1:46
Philip saith unto him, Come and see.

The quintessential invitation of apostolic faith: calling souls beyond intellectual debate into direct personal communion with the Savior.

Click to inscribe verse
Testimony III • Petition for the Beatific Vision John 14:8
Philip saith unto him, Lord, shew us the Father, and it sufficeth us.

Voicing humanity's eternal yearning to behold God, which drew forth Christ’s revelation: 'He that hath seen me hath seen the Father.'

Click to inscribe verse
Testimony IV • The Testing of Reliance John 6:5-7
Two hundred pennyworth of bread is not sufficient for them, that every one of them may take a little.

Acknowledging mortal limitation so that total faith may be reposed in the supernatural providence of the Son of God.

Click to inscribe verse
Testimony V • Opening the Sacred Scriptures Acts 8:30
And Philip ran thither to him, and heard him read the prophet Esaias, and said, Understandest thou what thou readest?

The apostolic duty of spiritual illumination, unlocking prophetic scripture to reveal Jesus as the suffering Servant and Redeemer.

Click to inscribe verse
Testimony VI • The Prerequisite of Holy Baptism Acts 8:36-37
And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest. And he answered and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.

Establishing undivided, wholehearted faith in Jesus Christ as the sacred condition for regeneration through water and the Spirit.

Click to inscribe verse
Testimony VII • The Oblation of the Martyr Acts of Philip 138
Lord Jesus Christ, Father of eternity, King of unquenchable light, into Thy holy hands I commend my spirit. Receive me into Thy heavenly sanctuary.

Surrendering life upon the gibbet in Hierapolis, yielding his soul unto the everlasting King of Light.
