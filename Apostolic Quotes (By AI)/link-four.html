Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Gospel of Matthew

Testimony I • The Law of Judgment Matthew 7:1-2
Judge not, that ye be not judged. For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.

The sovereign standard of divine reciprocity, demanding mercy and trembling humility from mortal men.

Testimony II • The Triad of Petition Matthew 7:7
Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.

The persistent invocation of divine mercy guaranteed to open the portals of heaven.

Testimony III • The Golden Rule Matthew 7:12
Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.

The pure summation of biblical righteousness and holy ethical conduct toward one's neighbor.

Testimony IV • Discernment of Fruits Matthew 7:15-16
Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves. Ye shall know them by their fruits.

Vigilance against spiritual deception, looking past deceptive outward forms unto true spiritual fruits.

Testimony V • The Strait Gate Matthew 7:13-14
Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat: Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

The solemn reality of eternity: the holy path of salvation is narrow, demanding vigilance and devotion.

Testimony VI • The House Upon the Rock Matthew 7:24-26
Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock... But every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand.

Unshakable spiritual foundation in active obedience to Christ amidst every storm and tempest.

Testimony VII • The Universal Promise Matthew 7:8
For everyone who asks receives; the one who seeks finds; and to the one who knocks, the door will be opened.

The unchanging assurance that divine grace is bestowed without failure on all who seek God in earnestness.
