    Apostle Peter Verses:

  • "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God." (Matthew 16:16)

  • "Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit." (Acts 2:38)

  • "We must obey God rather than men." (Acts 5:29)

  • "Silver or gold I do not have, but what I do have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ Nazarene, walk." (Acts 3:6)

  • "May your money perish with you, because you thought you could buy the gift of God with money!" (Acts 8:20)

  • "Humble yourselves, therefore, under God’s mighty hand, that he may lift you up in due time. Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you." (1 Peter 5:6-7)

  • "The Lord is not slow in keeping his promise, as some understand slowness. Instead he is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish, but everyone to come to repentance." (2 Peter 3:9)
