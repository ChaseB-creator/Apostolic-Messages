    Apostle John Verses:

  • John 1:1: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God," establishing Jesus' divinity.

  • John 14:6: Jesus declares, "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."

  • John 10:10: Jesus contrasts the thief's destructive intent with His own purpose: "I have come that they may have life, and have it to the full."

  • John 11:25: Jesus tells Martha, "I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die."

  • John 8:32: "Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."

  • John 15:13: "Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one’s life for one’s friends."

  • John 13:34: Jesus gives a new commandment: "Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another."
