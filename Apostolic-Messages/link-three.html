Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Epistle of James

Testimony I • The Living Faith James 2:17
Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.

The seminal doctrine that genuine saving faith inevitably bears the fruit of righteous action.

Testimony II • The Prayer for Wisdom James 1:5
If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

The gracious abundance of God, bestowing celestial discernment upon all who petition in faith.

Testimony III • Temperance of Spirit James 1:19
Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath.

The sacred discipline of the tongue and mastery of anger in walking with the brethren.

Testimony IV • Doers of the Word James 1:22
But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.

Warning against hollow intellectualism that listens to divine truth without obeying its call.

Testimony V • Righteous Intercession James 5:16
Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

The immense spiritual efficacy of mutual confession, humility, and steadfast supplication.

Testimony VI • Grace to the Humble James 4:6
God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble.

The unbending law of the Kingdom: spiritual elevation is granted only to the contrite heart.

Testimony VII • The Crown of Life James 1:12
Blessed is the man that endureth temptation: for when he is tried, he shall receive the crown of life, which the Lord hath promised to them that love him.

The eternal victor's wreath awaiting every soul that endures worldly tribulation for love of God.
