Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Exhortations of Jude

Testimony I • The Inquiry of Divine Manifestation John 14:22
Judas saith unto him, not Iscariot, Lord, how is it that thou wilt manifest thyself unto us, and not unto the world?

Inquiring into the profound mystery of interior spiritual manifestation reserved for obedient disciples.

Testimony II • Contending for the Apostolic Faith Jude 1:3
Beloved, when I gave all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, it was needful for me to write unto you, and exhort you that ye should earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints.

The clarion summons to defend the inviolable apostolic truth against deceptive heresies and apostasy.

Testimony III • The Fortress of the Soul Jude 1:20-21
But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost, keep yourselves in the love of God, looking for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life.

The fourfold spiritual stronghold: doctrinal foundation, charismatic prayer, abiding love, and expectation of eternal life.

Testimony IV • The Discernment of Compassion Jude 1:22-23
And of some have compassion, making a difference: and others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire; hating even the garment spotted by the flesh.

Balanced pastoral vigilance: tender mercy for struggling souls combined with fierce urgency to pluck sinners from destruction.

Testimony V • The Sovereign Preserving Power Jude 1:24
Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy.

The comforting promise of divine omnipotence able to preserve the believer spotless before the majestic throne of God.

Testimony VI • The Eternal Doxology Jude 1:25
To the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Amen.

Ascribed adoration to Jesus Christ as the only wise God and Savior, sovereign over all time and eternity.

Testimony VII • Apostolic Preaching in the East Acts of Thaddaeus / Apostolic Witness
I preach unto you Jesus Christ, the Son of God, who conquered death upon the wood and arose to deliver all who trust in Him from darkness into light everlasting.

Jude’s missionary testimony in Mesopotamia and Persia, sealing the gospel with his blood and martyrdom.
