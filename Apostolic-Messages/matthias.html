The Chosen Witness • Completer of the Twelve

Apostle Matthias Verses

"He who accompanied the Lord from the baptism of John unto the day of Ascension, chosen by solemn prayer and sacred lot to complete the apostolic Twelve and bear witness to the Resurrection."

Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Testimonies of Matthias

Testimony I • The Calling of the Resurrection Witness Acts 1:21-22
Wherefore of these men which have companied with us all the time that the Lord Jesus went in and out among us, beginning from the baptism of John, unto that same day that he was taken up from us, must one be ordained to be a witness with us of his resurrection.

The divine prerequisite of apostleship: steadfast eyewitness communion with the Lord and personal testimony of His bodily triumph over death.

Testimony II • The Prayer of Apostolic Consecration Acts 1:24-26
Thou, Lord, which knowest the hearts of all men, shew whether of these two thou hast chosen... And the lot fell upon Matthias; and he was numbered with the eleven apostles.

The sacred appointment of Matthias, demonstrating that holy ministry is conferred through divine omniscience and communal prayer.

Testimony III • The Contemplation of Holy Wonder Traditions of Matthias (Stromata II.9)
Wonder at that which is before you! For in holy wonder the soul awakens to behold the hidden majesty of God.

Matthias’s mystical maxim that reverent wonder before divine works is the threshold of celestial wisdom and intimate knowledge of God.

Testimony IV • Mortification and Spiritual Growth Traditions of Matthias (Stromata III.4)
We must strive against the flesh, yielding no license to its lusts, that our spirit may flourish through faith and heavenly knowledge.

Apostolic discipline subordinating bodily appetites to allow the inner man to grow in purity and communion with the Holy Spirit.

Testimony V • Communal Responsibility in Holiness Traditions of Matthias (Stromata VII.13)
If the neighbor of a believer stumbles into sin, the believer shares the burden; for had he walked worthily of Christ, his example would have turned his neighbor from iniquity.

The elevated apostolic standard of mutual accountability: our lives must shine with such radiance that they protect others from falling into sin.

Testimony VI • Shepherding the Afflicted Apostolic Preaching of Matthias
The Son of God called us not to rule over men in pride, but to be servants of the meek, comforters to the afflicted, and bearers of the light of truth.

Emphasizing the servant heart of Christ as the sole pattern for true apostolic leadership.

Testimony VII • The Crown of Martyrdom Martyrdom of St. Matthias
I praise Thee, O Lord Christ, who hast counted me worthy to bear Thy cross and to be numbered among Thy holy Twelve. Receive my spirit into Thy eternal peace.

Matthias’s final thanksgiving at his martyrdom in Colchis, finishing his course and joining the eternal company of the Apostles in glory.
