Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Proclamations of Andrew

Testimony I • The Finding of the Messiah John 1:41
We have found the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ.

The inaugural apostolic confession of faith, heralding the arrival of the promised Redeemer and bringing Simon Peter to Jesus.

Testimony II • The Offering in the Wilderness John 6:8-9
There is a lad here, which hath five barley loaves, and two small fishes: but what are they among so many?

Andrew humbly presents humanity's meager resources into the sovereign hands of Christ, preparing the way for divine multiplication.

Testimony III • The Gentile Intercession John 12:20-22
Philip cometh and telleth Andrew: and again Andrew and Philip tell Jesus: Sir, we would see Jesus.

Interceding as an apostolic bridge for seekers among the Gentile nations to behold the Savior before His Passion.

Testimony IV • The Soliloquy to the Sacred Cross Passion of St. Andrew 10
O cross most welcome and long-desired, take me from among men and restore me to my Master, that by thee He may receive me who by thee redeemed me.

Embracing the instrument of martyrdom with ecstatic devotion, beholding the cross not as ruin, but as the royal ladder unto Christ.

Testimony V • Boldness Before the Tyrant Acts of Andrew 14
If I feared the terror of the cross, I would not preach the majesty of the cross. For the mystery of the cross surpasses all earthly glory.

Apostolic fearlessness confronting worldly persecution, professing the transcendent triumph of Christ crucified over all empire.

Testimony VI • The Altar of the Living Lamb Martyrdom of Andrew 5
Every day upon the altar of the living God, I offer not the flesh of bulls or blood of goats, but the spotless Lamb that was slain, whose body and blood feed the faithful.

The eternal memorial of the Lamb of God whose living presence sanctifies and nourishes the Church throughout all generations.

Testimony VII • Dying Blessing upon the Flock Apostolic Traditions of Andrew
Stand fast, my beloved brethren, in the faith once delivered to you; love one another, and let no worldly terror shake your confidence in Jesus Christ.

A shepherd’s parting testament from the cross, charging the flock to persevere in love, unwavering faith, and heavenly fortitude.
