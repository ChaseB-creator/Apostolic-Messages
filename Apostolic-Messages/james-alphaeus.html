Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Decrees of James the Less

Testimony I • The Council Decree of Grace Acts 15:19-20
Wherefore my sentence is, that we trouble not them, which from among the Gentiles are turned to God: but that we write unto them, that they abstain from pollutions of idols, and from fornication, and from things strangled, and from blood.

The landmark apostolic decree at the Council of Jerusalem, opening the door of salvation to the Gentile nations through faith in Christ.

Testimony II • Discerning the Divine Harvest Acts 15:13-14
And after they had held their peace, James answered, saying, Men and brethren, hearken unto me: Simeon hath declared how God at the first did visit the Gentiles, to take out of them a people for his name.

Discerning God's eternal redemptive covenant to gather a royal priesthood from every kindred, tongue, and nation.

Testimony III • The Multitude of the Faithful Acts 21:20
And when they heard it, they glorified the Lord, and said unto him, Thou seest, brother, how many thousands of Jews there are which believe; and they are all zealous of the law.

Celebrating the outpouring of divine grace in Jerusalem, where thousands embraced the Lord while keeping covenant reverence.

Testimony IV • The Marks of Celestial Wisdom James 3:17
But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

Delineating the holy qualities of heaven-sent wisdom that govern the minds and hearts of true disciples.

Testimony V • Patient Endurance of the Coming James 5:7-8
Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord. Behold, the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth... Be ye also patient; stablish your hearts: for the coming of the Lord draweth nigh.

Exhorting the Church to anchor its soul in steadfast patience, awaiting the imminent return of the King of Glory.

Testimony VI • Proclamation at the Temple Pinnacle Hegesippus / Eusebius II.23
Why do ye ask me concerning Jesus the Son of Man? He Himself sitteth in heaven at the right hand of the Great Power, and shall come on the clouds of heaven!

James's majestic confession before the scribes and Pharisees, proclaiming Christ's enthronement and heavenly return.

Testimony VII • The Final Martyr’s Intercession Eusebius II.23
I beseech Thee, Lord God and Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do.

Echoing the cross in his dying breath, James intercedes with divine charity for those who cast him down from the temple parapet.
