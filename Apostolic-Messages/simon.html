Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Proclamations of Simon the Zealot

Testimony I • The Renunciation of Earthly Swords Apostolic Confession of Simon
No mortal sword or worldly empire can redeem the soul of man. Jesus Christ alone is our King, and His Kingdom is not of this world.

Simon's radical conversion from violent political rebellion to joyful obedience under the spiritual reign of Christ.

Testimony II • Zeal for the Holy Sanctuary John 2:17 / Apostolic Meditation
The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up. In Christ, my fiery zeal is no longer for earthly vengeance, but for the purity of God’s living temple.

Consecrating human zeal into a blazing fire of holiness for the cleansing of the heart and the Church.

Testimony III • Spiritual Warfare Above Worldly Might Passion of Simon and Jude
We come not with weapons of brass or iron, but bearing the invincible sign of the cross to dispel demons and bring peace unto your souls.

Proclaiming that spiritual liberation through Christ far exceeds the vain conquests of human princes and legions.

Testimony IV • The Sanctification of Zeal through Charity Apostolic Preaching of Simon
If a disciple burn with zeal yet lack the charity of Christ, his fire consumeth his brethren rather than blessing them. Love is the fulfillment of all holy zeal.

Establishing divine love as the essential compass governing all apostolic action and spiritual passion.

Testimony V • Fervency in Spiritual Service Romans 12:11 / Apostolic Admonition
Not slothful in business; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord; rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation; continuing instant in prayer.

Calling believers to unquenchable enthusiasm and endurance, refusing spiritual apathy in the service of the King.

Testimony VI • Unshakeable Defiance of Death Martyrdom of St. Simon
You may cut down this mortal flesh, but you cannot separate my soul from the love of Jesus Christ my Lord.

Simon's triumphant fortitude under horrific martyrdom, holding fast to the promise of eternal resurrection.

Testimony VII • The Sovereign Triumph of the Cross Doxology of Simon the Zealot
Unto Him who broke the bonds of sin upon the tree and established peace between heaven and earth, be glory and honor forever and ever. Amen.

Exalting the cross of Christ as the true instrument of cosmic peace and divine victory.
