Seven Consecrated Testimonies

The Proclamations of Peter

Testimony I • The Great Confession Matthew 16:16
You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.

The bedrock revelation revealed not by flesh and blood, but by the Father in heaven.

Testimony II • The Call to Repentance Acts 2:38
Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

The fiery Pentecostal sermon commanding turning of the heart and the indwelling of the Spirit.

Testimony III • Supreme Allegiance Acts 5:29
We must obey God rather than men.

Steadfast apostolic boldness defying earthly persecution in devotion to heavenly truth.

Testimony IV • Power Above Silver Acts 3:6
Silver or gold I do not have, but what I do have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ Nazarene, walk.

Demonstrating that the divine authority of Christ's name transcends all temporal riches.

Testimony V • Holy Rebuke Acts 8:20
May your money perish with you, because you thought you could buy the gift of God with money!

The stern defense of divine grace against commercial defilement and simony.

Testimony VI • The Mighty Hand 1 Peter 5:6-7
Humble yourselves, therefore, under God’s mighty hand, that he may lift you up in due time. Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you.

Pastoral consolation anchoring the burdened heart in the protective providence of the Lord.

Testimony VII • Divine Patience 2 Peter 3:9
The Lord is not slow in keeping his promise, as some understand slowness. Instead he is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish, but everyone to come to repentance.

The boundless forbearance of the Almighty, desiring salvation for every soul.
